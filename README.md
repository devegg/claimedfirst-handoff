# ClaimedFirst

**Find them early. Choose your roster. Build your reputation.**

ClaimedFirst is an idea for a social discovery game about AI music creators. Players have a limited number of artists they can actively support, make deliberate roster choices, and earn recognition as those artists gain supporters.

**Status (2026-10-02): a working app exists, the idea is set down, and it is being offered to a developer.** Start with [docs/handoff/HANDOFF.md](docs/handoff/HANDOFF.md).

## Why share this?

Brian Boyd originated the idea and would love to see it become a working app. He is not seeking payment, ownership of someone else's implementation, or a commitment to involve him. Recognition and occasional updates would be appreciated, but are not intended requirements. He is not committing to build, fund, or operate the app.

The intended public release will invite independent developers to adapt and commercialize the project. The project is released under the MIT license (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), so a developer is free to adapt it, rename it and commercialize it.

## The game in one minute

You discover a promising AI musician. Your roster has one open slot. You claim the artist and become supporter #7 within ClaimedFirst. As more players discover that artist, your early choice contributes to your seasonal performance. Later, another artist catches your ear: watch them, replace a current pick, or consider a larger roster.

Your discovery history records the artists you believed in. Seasonal scoreboards give those choices a competitive context.

## Important boundaries

- Limited support rosters and gamification are central.
- Paid roster expansion is part of the original concept and a potential way for a developer to fund operations, their time, and possible seasonal prizes.
- Separate scoreboards and weighted or normalized scores are options, not settled rules.
- The game should operate independently of generator APIs, external play counts, embedded players, or platform partnerships.
- AI music creators are the subject of the game. Generator support does not guarantee permanent access to their music.
- SpinRoom is a separate, full listening-party platform. It is available to the right developer and is not part of this handoff's core scope.
- No affiliation with Suno or another platform is implied.
