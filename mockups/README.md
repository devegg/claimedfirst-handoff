# ClaimedFirst interactive concept

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. It is self-contained and needs no installation, account, API, or build.

## Walkthrough

1. Meet Ember Vale and claim your fifth slot.
2. Explore Juniper Static and try the full-roster choices: watch, replace, or expand.
3. Compare capacity divisions with an average-score alternative in The season.
4. Preview the original roster tiers and visit My discoveries.
5. Use Reset demo to start again.

All content and scores are fictional. State lives only in memory. Audio, payments, prizes, backend storage, full scoring, cooldown enforcement, and permanent history are not implemented. The division and average-score screens are alternative mockups, not final rules. Upgrades illustrate next-season division changes.

Visual direction: dark record-shop atmosphere, editorial headings, chartreuse accents, and original geometric cover treatments. No external assets or services are loaded.
