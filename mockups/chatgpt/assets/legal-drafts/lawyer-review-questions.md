# Ten questions for a lawyer before launch
1. Can a permanent claim number and date remain after account deletion? Which deletion option is lawful and workable?
2. Does listing a real artist’s name and public profile without permission create name, identity, or other rights issues?
3. What process is needed for impersonation, ownership disputes, takedowns, and appeals? Is the proposed 72-hour window appropriate?
4. Could the claim and points game be treated as gambling, a contest, or a prize promotion, even with no prizes or payment?
5. How would later paid slots, cosmetics, artist tools, or other paid perks change the Terms, disclosures, or legal analysis?
6. Which minimum age and privacy laws apply to the places where the site will be available?
7. Do the three claim visibility levels, private artist audience list, public numbers, and share links give users an accurate privacy choice?
8. What content permission, moderation rules, and complaint procedure are needed for submitted artist names and links?
9. What disclosures and contracts are needed for hosting, sign-in email, future sign-in providers, cookies, analytics, referrals, and outbound campaign tags?
10. Who should be the legal operator, what governing law and dispute process should apply, and what liability limits are appropriate?
