### What we collect and why

We store your email address to send sign-in links and run your account. We store your chosen handle for your profile and the parts of the site where you choose to show it.

We store claims, their numbers and dates, whether they are active or historical, and your privacy choice for each claim. We store your watchlist and your privacy choice for each watch. We store referral links and referral activity to operate the referral feature. We store points to operate the monthly board.

If you submit or manage an artist page, we store the artist name and links you provide. This may include song titles, song links, and an optional donation link. ### Who can see it

Your handle is public. For each claim, you can choose **Show my name**, **Artist only**, or **Anonymous**. With **Show my name**, your handle may appear with the claim on public boards and your public profile, and the verified artist can see it. With **Artist only**, the verified artist can see your handle, but the public sees an anonymous claim. With **Anonymous**, the verified artist and public do not see your handle with that claim. The claim number still counts in each case. [CONFIRM WHICH CLAIM DATES AND STATUSES APPEAR PUBLICLY.]

The public never sees your watchlist or the names of watchers. For each watch, you choose whether the verified artist can see your handle. Only a verified artist can see their private audience list. Anonymous people remain anonymous in that list. The artist can see audience totals.

A link or image you choose to share may reveal a claim to people who receive it. [CONFIRM HOW LATER PRIVACY CHANGES AFFECT OLD LINKS AND SAVED IMAGES.]
