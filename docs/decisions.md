# Direction and open decisions

Updated September 26, 2026. Current user clarifications take precedence over earlier recommendations.

## Confirmed direction

- We are providing an original idea, a working prototype, and the design documents behind it for someone else to develop.
- Brian is not committing to build, fund, or operate a working app.
- Gamification and a limited active-support roster are central to ClaimedFirst.
- Paid roster expansion belongs in the concept and could fund costs, developer time, and possible seasonal prizes.
- Separate scoreboards by capacity and weighted-average scoring are options Brian wants considered.
- Competition rules remain open; suggestions must not become requirements by accident.
- ClaimedFirst should be independently buildable without relying on changes to Suno or another generator's APIs, access, or platform terms.
- Music appreciation focuses on AI creators; no permanent guarantee of external music access is assumed.
- SpinRoom is a separate listening-party idea that may be suitable for generator integration.
- Work begins privately on GitHub and can become public when the handoff is ready.
- Brian seeks no compensation. Recognition and updates are welcome, not intended conditions of adoption.

## Original concept, preserved for reference

Limited rosters, paid capacity tiers, permanent first-claim history, different active positions on return, a 30-day cooldown, early-claim growth multipliers, loyalty, watchlists, seasons, scout reputation, and sharing.

The archive remains verbatim. Its assertion that the concept is validated is not evidence of demand; its technical and anti-abuse claims are not implementation guarantees.

## Options rather than decisions

- Capacity divisions, averages, weighted averages, fixed seasonal lineups, or multiple boards.
- Final tier pricing and whether the ten-slot tier is free.
- Original cooldown versus transfers, locks, or other replacement rules.
- Season duration, rollover, upgrades, downgrades, and slot expiry.
- Recognition-only rewards, cosmetics, sponsored rewards, or funded prizes.
- External references versus optional authorized audio hosting.
- Claim, Support, or Champion as the action label.
- Visual direction and the illustrative ruleset used in mockups.

## Superseded assistant recommendations

The earlier recommendation to center a general social community, omit scoring, remove paid roster expansion, and make listening parties the main mockup journey does not represent the current brief.

An earlier document also treated fixed equal scoring capacity as the preferred answer. It is now one option among several, not a restriction on the adopter's business model.

## Before public release

Choose file licensing, check asset permissions and history, decide preview hosting and contact preferences, and review the invitation. No affiliation with a generator is implied. Do not change visibility or send a community message until Brian is ready.

## Builder questions

How is growth counted? How do newcomers compete? Can a formula be manipulated by changing capacity or dropping artists? How are duplicate artists and fabricated supporters handled? Who covers operating costs and moderation? What happens to unavailable music and deleted accounts? Which board, if any, determines prizes?

These questions help someone take the idea further; answering all of them is not required before showing static mockups.
