# ClaimedFirst teaser video: design brief and prompt

For a free AI video generation. All people, artists and numbers are fictional. Tool-agnostic: paste the prompt into whichever generator you use, and add the on-screen text in an editor afterward (AI video tools usually draw text badly).

## Brief

- **Goal:** make someone feel "I want my number." One idea only: the thrill of being early, and the number that proves it.
- **Length:** 15 to 20 seconds is the safest for a free generation. A 30 second cut is described as an option.
- **Format:** vertical 9:16 for stories, plus a square 1:1 crop if possible.
- **Audience:** music fans who discover small artists.
- **Feeling:** a dimly lit record shop just before a show. Anticipation, not hype. Editorial, warm, intimate. Not a tech-startup look, no purple gradients, no stock-market imagery (no charts, tickers, coins or trading screens).
- **Palette:** near-black charcoal, warm paper cream, one vermilion-red accent.
- **Sound:** soft vinyl crackle, then a mellow dream-pop guitar phrase fading in. No vocals, no voiceover.
- **Do not show:** real brands, logos, real artists, readable app screens, or any text generated by the model.

## Shot list (20 seconds)

1. **0-4s:** Extreme close-up of a vinyl record spinning, tonearm lowering in dark warm light. Dust drifts in a lamp beam. A thin red label glints.
2. **4-8s:** A hand slides a record out of a crate full of unlabeled sleeves, one sleeve slightly out of line. Shallow depth of field.
3. **8-13s:** Over the shoulder of a person in a small crowd at a tiny stage. A single performer is half-lit, faces in the room turn toward them. Someone near the front is smiling as if they knew.
4. **13-17s:** Close-up on a hand holding a phone, screen glowing but blurred and unreadable. A small red number-shaped glow, no legible text, pulses on the glass.
5. **17-20s:** Cut to black with the record still turning in the lower corner. Hold.

## On-screen text to add in an editor

1. At 13s: a large cream "#7" in a serif display font with a vermilion accent.
2. At 17s: "I found them first." (small, centered)
3. At 19s: "ClaimedFirst" wordmark, small, with "Coming soon" beneath.

## Prompt (paste this)

> A 20-second vertical cinematic teaser, shot like a quiet music documentary. Dim, warm, intimate record shop and a tiny live room. Shot 1: extreme close-up of a vinyl record spinning as a tonearm lowers, dust floating in a lamp beam. Shot 2: a hand slides a record from a crate of unlabeled sleeves, shallow depth of field. Shot 3: over-the-shoulder view of a small crowd watching a half-lit solo performer on a tiny stage, a few faces smiling as if they knew this artist before anyone else. Shot 4: close-up of a hand holding a glowing phone, the screen blurred and unreadable. Shot 5: fade to black with the record still turning. Color palette: near-black charcoal, warm cream, a single vermilion-red accent. Soft film grain, slow gentle camera moves, 24 fps, anamorphic feel. Audio: vinyl crackle and a mellow dream-pop guitar phrase, no vocals. No text, no logos, no brand names, no charts, no money imagery, no purple or neon.

## Short prompt (if the tool has a small limit)

> Dim record shop, vinyl spinning, a hand picks a record from a crate, a small crowd watches a half-lit solo performer, glowing blurred phone, fade to black. Charcoal, cream and one vermilion accent. Slow camera, film grain, vinyl crackle and soft guitar. No text, no logos.

## Negative prompt (if the tool has one)

> text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, brand names, stock charts, coins, dollar signs, neon, purple gradient, cartoon, extra fingers, distorted faces

## If the first result is off

1. **Faces look strange:** replace shot 3 with the back of heads and a silhouetted performer.
2. **Phone screen shows text:** make it a close-up of a hand holding a glowing red light instead, and add "#7" in the editor.
3. **Too busy:** keep only shots 1, 2 and 5, and add the text over shot 2.

## A 30-second cut

Add 5 seconds before shot 3: a nighttime street, someone listening through earbuds and stopping mid-step. Add 5 seconds after shot 4: the same hand sends the phone's glow to a friend's phone across the table, and the friend leans in. Add one more on-screen line at 25s: "Be #8."
