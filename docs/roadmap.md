# Possible next steps for a new owner

These are suggestions only. Brian is not building, running or scheduling any of this, and nothing here is a commitment.

1. Decide the rules: roster size, scoring, any divisions, transfers, and the business model. Simulate how a few kinds of players would fare before building more.
2. Keep artist identity tied to a public profile link, so a broken music link does not break the game.
3. Run the first test with about 30 consenting AI music creators, and count how many come back on another day and back a different artist.
4. Test abuse cases early: coordinated accounts and claim cycling (see `docs/trial-safety.md`).
5. Decide pricing, hosting and moderation, and whether any reward or prize is appropriate, before making any offer to users.
6. Refine from real use and from what it actually costs to run.

SpinRoom, a separate listening-party platform, is available to the right developer and is independent of ClaimedFirst. ClaimedFirst does not need to wait for a generator partnership.
