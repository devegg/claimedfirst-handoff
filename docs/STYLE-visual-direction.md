# How this material looks

A companion to the writing style page. This one describes the visual treatment
every asset in this set shares. It is a design reference, not subject matter.
Nothing on this page is a topic to cover, narrate, describe, or read aloud, and
no output should ever mention colors, fonts, or layout.

## Feeling

A dimly lit record shop before the show starts. Dark, warm, editorial,
designed by someone who loves music. Not a tech-startup look and not a finance
dashboard.

## Palette

Dark green-black with warm paper-white text and one coral accent, matching the
website.

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Background | `#121613` — deep green-black, never pure black |
| Panel | `#1B211C` |
| Secondary panel | `#252D26` |
| Lines and borders | `#3E4A40` |
| Text | `#F3EFE5` — warm paper white, never pure white |
| Muted text | `#BAC4B8` |
| Accent | `#E48C72` — coral, for the one thing that matters most on a screen |

Coral is the emphasis color and it earns its place by being scarce: one coral
element per scene, usually the claim number. No purple gradients, no neon, no
gold, no trophy yellow.

## Typography

Headings and the big claim number in Fraunces, a soft serif with character.
Body text in DM Sans, clean and readable. Generous spacing. Nothing condensed,
nothing italicized for emphasis. Text is the content, not decoration over an
image, and every word must be legible at the size it will be viewed.

## Illustration

Flat, calm and graphic. The recurring image is a record: concentric groove
rings with a small label disc, drawn in line work and simple shapes. The claim
number lands like a stamp on a ticket. Placeholder artwork only.

No photographs, no stock-photo fans with headphones, no glowing waveforms, no
three-dimensional renders. People, if shown, are simple figures at a show or
with a phone, drawn plainly.

## Motion

Slow and deliberate. A number stamps in once. A card slides up once. No
flashing, no spinning, no rapid cuts. Everything respects a viewer who prefers
less motion.

## Density

Fewer elements, more space. One idea per scene. Prefer five clear scenes over
twelve crowded ones, and every extra word rendered inside artwork is another
chance for a spelling error.

## What never appears

No real artist names, artwork, logos, or screenshots of other services. No
Suno or YouTube branding. No company names, contact details or website
addresses. No prizes, trophies, medals, money, or price tags. No stock-market
imagery: no tickers, candlestick charts, upward arrows, bulls, or coins. No
invented statistics, including numbers used as decoration; the only numbers
are the example claim numbers such as #7.
